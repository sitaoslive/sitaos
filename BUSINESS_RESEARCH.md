## 2. PRIMARY BEACHHEAD DEEP-DIVE: US HVAC & HOME SERVICES

### The Verified Problem
Small to medium HVAC and plumbing businesses (2–15 technicians) lose 25% to 40% of their new revenue opportunities due to:
1. **Unanswered After-Hours Calls & Inquiries:** Homeowners with broken heating or plumbing call the next company if not answered in under 5 minutes.
2. **Unfollowed Estimates:** Technicians produce quotes ($2,500 to $12,000 for system replacements), but the owner or office staff fails to systematically follow up 48h, 5 days, and 10 days later.

- **Smith.ai:** $285 to $1,950/month (Call answering only, limited CRM sync).

- **ServiceTitan:** $150 to $400/tech/month (Complex ERP, inaccessible to small contractors).
