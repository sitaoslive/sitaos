🛠️ Module 3: Error Checking & Troubleshooting Playbook

When something looks wrong, consult this guide. 99% of issues fall into one of these verified scenarios:

SCENARIO 1

"The site sitaos.com is slow to load the first time"

Why it happens: On Render's Free tier, the web service spins down after 15 minutes of inactivity. The first visitor triggers a "cold start" taking ~30–45 seconds.

The Fix: Once loaded, it stays fast! To prevent sleep, ping it once every 10 minutes via cron, or upgrade Render to Starter ($7/mo) for 0ms cold starts.

SCENARIO 2

"A lead texted STOP and received no further messages"

Why it happens: This is a feature, not a bug! Our Compliance Policy Gate automatically registers an opt-out across SMS and WhatsApp to protect against TCPA fines ($1,500/msg).

The Fix: No action needed. If the lead changes their mind, they must text START to reactivate.

SCENARIO 3

"AI stopped replying to a prospect in GoHighLevel"

Why it happens: A human agent sent a message, triggering Human Takeover Lock (human_takeover_active: true).

The Fix: Either let the 60-minute timer expire naturally, or remove the human_takeover_active tag in GoHighLevel to hand control back to the AI immediately.

SCENARIO 4

"WhatsApp message failed with '24-hour window expired'"

Why it happens: Official Meta policy requires that messages sent more than 24 hours after the user's last message MUST use an approved Utility Template.

The Fix: Go to sitaos.com/whatsapp and send an official approved template like avm_onboarding_reminder_v1 to re-open the 24h window.
